Authentic South Indian, On Wheels

Gowda's Food is a brand-new South Indian food truck now serving Knoxville. We're bringing the soul of real South Indian cooking, from crisp dosas and fluffy idli to golden bondas and crispy vada, straight to your neighbourhood.

Everything is made with hand-ground spices and cherished family recipes, cooked fresh to order. This isn't fast food. It's tradition, served with love. Come visit us!
