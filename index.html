Grand Opening · October 15

Authentic South Indian, On Wheels

Gowda's Food is a brand-new South Indian food truck rolling into Knoxville this month. We're bringing the soul of real South Indian cooking, from crisp dosas and fluffy idli to fragrant biryani and golden bondas, straight to your neighbourhood.

Everything is made with hand-ground spices and cherished family recipes, cooked fresh to order. This isn't fast food. It's tradition, served with love. Come celebrate our grand opening on October 15th!

  • ✦ Hand-ground spice blends, made fresh daily
  • ✦ Cherished family recipes, cooked to order
  • ✦ Locally sourced, seasonal ingredients
